CurateTube

Organize, analyze, and curate your YouTube playlists with ease.

What is CurateTube?

CurateTube is a browser extension designed to help YouTube users better manage and understand their playlists.

As YouTube collections grow, it can become difficult to remember what videos are in different playlists, identify common themes, or organize content into useful collections. CurateTube provides tools that make this process easier.

The extension connects to your YouTube account through Google's authentication system and uses the YouTube APIs to access the playlist information required for the features you choose to use.

What Can CurateTube Do?

Playlist Analysis

CurateTube can analyze the videos and metadata within your YouTube playlists to help you understand the content in your collections.

Playlist Organization

The extension can help organize YouTube playlists based on the content and metadata associated with the videos.

Recommendations

CurateTube can generate recommendations for organizing and curating playlists based on the videos and collections you choose to analyze.

YouTube Playlist Management

When explicitly requested by the user, CurateTube can create, modify, or organize YouTube playlists using the permissions granted through Google authentication.
