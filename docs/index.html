CurateTube

A browser extension for analyzing, organizing, and managing YouTube playlists.

Purpose of CurateTube

CurateTube is a browser extension that connects to a user's YouTube account to analyze, organize, and manage YouTube playlists.

It helps users understand the videos in their playlists, identify patterns in their collections, generate playlist recommendations, and organize their YouTube content more effectively.

CurateTube uses Google's authentication system and YouTube APIs to access the YouTube playlist information required to provide these features. Actions that modify a user's playlists are performed only when explicitly requested by the user.

What is CurateTube?

CurateTube is designed to help YouTube users better manage and understand their playlists.

As YouTube collections grow, it can become difficult to remember what videos are in different playlists, identify common themes, or organize content into useful collections. CurateTube provides tools that make this process easier.
