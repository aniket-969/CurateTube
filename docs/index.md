# CurateTube Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 17, 2026**

CurateTube is a browser extension that helps users analyze their YouTube playlists and create or organize playlists.

We respect your privacy. CurateTube does not operate a backend server, does not maintain user accounts, and does not store your personal data on our servers.

## SECTION 1 - INFORMATION CURATETUBE ACCESSES

When you use CurateTube, the extension may access information from your YouTube account through the YouTube APIs after you authenticate with Google.

This may include:

- Your YouTube playlists
- Playlist titles, descriptions, and identifiers
- Videos contained in your playlists
- Video metadata such as titles, channels, and other publicly available metadata
- Information required to create or modify YouTube playlists when you explicitly request those actions

CurateTube only requests access necessary for the features provided by the extension.

## SECTION 2 - HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS USED

Information accessed by CurateTube is used solely to provide the functionality of the extension, including:

- Reading and analyzing your YouTube playlists
- Analyzing video metadata
- Generating playlist recommendations
- Creating or organizing YouTube playlists at your request
- Updating playlists when you explicitly choose to do so

CurateTube does not use your YouTube data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

## SECTION 3 - DATA STORAGE

CurateTube does not operate a backend server or database for storing user information.

Playlist data and analysis performed by the extension are processed locally within the extension unless transmission to a third-party service is required to provide a specific feature.

Any data stored locally by the extension is stored on your own device and is not accessible to CurateTube.

## SECTION 4 - THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

CurateTube may communicate directly with third-party services required to provide its functionality.

### Google / YouTube

CurateTube uses Google's authentication and YouTube APIs to allow you to access and manage your YouTube playlists.

Your use of Google and YouTube services is also subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

### AI / LLM Services

CurateTube uses a third-party AI service to analyze video or playlist metadata and generate classifications or recommendations.

When this functionality is used, relevant video or playlist metadata are transmitted directly from the extension to the applicable AI service.

CurateTube does not operate a server that receives or stores this information before it is sent to the AI service.

## SECTION 5 - DATA SHARING

CurateTube does not sell, rent, or otherwise monetize your personal information.

CurateTube does not maintain a database of user information or YouTube account data.

Information is transmitted directly to third-party services only when necessary to provide functionality requested by the user, such as Google/YouTube APIs or AI services used for playlist analysis.

## SECTION 6 - AUTHENTICATION

CurateTube uses Google authentication to obtain the permissions required to interact with your YouTube account.

CurateTube does not receive or store your Google account password.

Authentication and authorization are handled through Google's authentication mechanisms.

## SECTION 7 - SECURITY

Because CurateTube does not operate a backend or maintain a database containing user information, it does not retain user data on its own infrastructure.

We nevertheless take reasonable measures in the extension's implementation to limit access to information to what is necessary for the extension's functionality.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your own Google account and device.

## SECTION 8 - COOKIES AND TRACKING

CurateTube does not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

CurateTube does not use your YouTube activity to build an advertising profile or track you across websites.

Third-party services used by the extension, including Google and any AI service, may have their own privacy practices and technologies that are outside CurateTube's control.

## SECTION 9 - DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

CurateTube does not maintain user data on its own servers.

Because there is no CurateTube account or backend database, there is no CurateTube account data that needs to be deleted from our servers.

You may stop the extension from accessing your YouTube account by revoking CurateTube's Google authorization through your Google account settings or by uninstalling the extension.

Data maintained by Google, YouTube, or other third-party services is subject to their respective policies and controls.

## SECTION 11 - CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as CurateTube's functionality changes.

Any changes will be reflected in the updated version of this policy, together with a revised "Last updated" date.
